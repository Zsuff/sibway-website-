Sibway Logistics — a logistics partner for your business

Sibway Logistics is a logistics company that arranges international freight transport for businesses between Ukraine and the countries of Europe. We handle palletised and non-palletised cargo, hazardous and sensitive cargo, and take account of weight, volume, packaging type and delivery deadlines.

Our clients order freight transport between Ukraine and Europe to import and export goods. We select the transport format, route and vehicle type for each client's specific task. Our goal is to build a long-term partnership, so we treat every enquiry carefully and communicate openly at every stage of the shipment.
