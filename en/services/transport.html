What we offer

LTL (consolidated cargo)

A cost-effective solution for smaller shipments. You pay only for the space your cargo occupies. Ideal for 1–10 pallets.

FTL (full truckload)

A dedicated vehicle for your cargo. Best for large shipments or where the fastest possible delivery is required.

Temperature-controlled transport

Cargo transport with the required temperature regime. Suitable for food, pharmaceuticals and chemicals.

Hazardous goods (ADR)

Delivery of ADR-class cargo in compliance with all safety requirements.

Pricing factors

The cost of international transport depends on:

  • Cargo type — standard, temperature-controlled, hazardous (ADR) or oversized
  • Weight and volume — affect vehicle selection and the amount of cargo space required
  • Route — distance, number of borders and customs checkpoints
  • Urgency — standard or express delivery
  • Additional services — customs clearance, insurance, loading/unloading
  • Seasonality — peak periods (October–December) may be more expensive

Transport documents

Minimum package:

  • Invoice
  • Packing list
  • Contract with the supplier/buyer
  • CMR consignment note

Additional documents, if required

  • Certificates of conformity
  • Transport permits for ADR or oversized cargo
  • Customs declaration
  • Phytosanitary certificates for food products

Frequently asked questions

How can I calculate the transport cost?

The price depends on weight, volume, distance and cargo type. Send us an enquiry and we will prepare a quote within the day.

Which documents are required for customs clearance?

An invoice, packing list, contract and, where necessary, certificate of origin. We will provide the complete list after reviewing your cargo.

Can I track my cargo during transport?

Yes. We provide a tracking number and update statuses at every delivery stage.

How long does delivery from Poland take?

Standard delivery takes 3–4 days for LTL and 2–3 days for FTL.

Do you transport hazardous goods?

Yes. We arrange and deliver ADR-class cargo in compliance with all safety requirements.

How does payment work?

Payment is made by invoice to a bank account. Prepayment or post-payment may be available by agreement.
