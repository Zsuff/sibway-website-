Warehousing services

We provide warehousing services for businesses in Ukraine and the EU: temporary storage, cargo consolidation, cross-docking and tailored solutions for complex cargo.

Pricing factors

The cost of warehousing services depends on:

  • Storage area — square metres or pallet spaces
  • Storage period — the longer the period, the more favourable the daily rate
  • Cargo type — standard, temperature-controlled, hazardous or fragile
  • Additional services — loading/unloading, packing and labelling
  • Warehouse location — warehouses in large cities may cost more

Required documents

Minimum package:

  • Warehousing services agreement
  • Invoice
  • Packing list
  • CMR consignment note

Additional documents, if required

  • Certificates of conformity
  • Storage permits for ADR cargo or food products
  • Insurance policy

Working example

Consolidation of goods from Germany for an online store

Cargo
5 consignments from different suppliers, 45 pallets, 8,500 kg
Timeframe
5 days (collection + consolidation + delivery)

Solution

  • Collection of cargo at a warehouse in Wrocław, Poland
  • Consolidation into one shipment
  • FTL transport to Kyiv

Result: The client received all goods in one shipment and saved 35% on delivery costs.

Frequently asked questions

How can I calculate storage costs?

The price depends on the area, period and cargo type. Send an enquiry and we will prepare a quote within the day.

Can I visit the warehouse?

Yes. By prior arrangement, you can visit the warehouse and check the cargo condition.

Do you insure cargo?

Yes. We offer cargo insurance for the full value.

What are the conditions for temperature-controlled cargo?

We have warehouses with temperature control from -20°C to +25°C.

Do you work with private individuals?

Yes. We provide warehousing services to individuals on tailored terms.

How does payment work?

Payment is made by invoice to a bank account. Monthly payment based on actual storage is available.
