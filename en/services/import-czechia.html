Import Freight Transport from Czechia to Ukraine for Business

Sibway Logistics arranges import freight transport from Czechia to Ukraine for companies buying goods, materials, components or equipment from Czech suppliers.

We agree an FTL, LTL or pallet format based on your batch's parameters and your delivery's needs. We often arrange import transport from Prague, Brno and Ostrava to Ukraine.

Who this service is for

Transport from Czechia to Ukraine is designed for companies buying goods from Czech manufacturers, suppliers or distributors.

The service suits:

  • importers of goods from Czechia;
  • manufacturing and trading companies needing materials, components or finished products;
  • distributors restocking their warehouse or arranging regular purchases;
  • companies working with one or several Czech suppliers;
  • procurement and logistics specialists planning commercial transport to Ukraine;
  • businesses that need a full, consolidated or pallet load.

We work with B2B shipments.

What we transport

FTL — full truckload

FTL Czechia–Ukraine suits large batches, regular deliveries, industrial cargo, equipment, construction materials, goods for distribution and other cargo that needs a dedicated truck.

A full truckload format can also make sense if the cargo has significant dimensions, specific packaging, several loading points or needs its own delivery schedule.

LTL — consolidated cargo

LTL, or consolidated format, is used when a batch doesn't fill a whole vehicle. This can be palletised goods, auto parts, medical goods, energy equipment, food products, construction materials or other commercial cargo that can be transported in a consolidated format based on its parameters and conditions.

Before agreeing on LTL we check the cargo's compatibility with other shipments, any required temperature regime, packaging requirements and other transport conditions.

Pallet shipments

For a pallet shipment we need the number of pallets, their height, weight, packaging type, stacking possibility, loading conditions and the delivery point in Ukraine. This data determines the transport format and route.

Cargo with special parameters. If the cargo has non-standard dimensions, significant weight, specific packaging or special loading conditions, send us the parameters, photos or a technical specification. We'll assess the possible transport format and the list of data to confirm before the route starts. By separate agreement we arrange transport of oversized cargo, ADR goods and temperature-controlled cargo. Such deliveries need a preliminary assessment of parameters, transport requirements and documents.

How the transport works

  1. 1. You send a request

    Tell us the city or loading address in Czechia — including Prague, Brno, Ostrava or another city — the delivery location in Ukraine, the cargo description and its readiness date.

  2. 2. We clarify the cargo and route parameters

    For the quote we clarify weight, volume, dimensions, number of pallets or items, packaging type, loading conditions, the need for a temperature regime or special transport, and the number of suppliers.

  3. 3. We agree the transport format

    We agree an FTL, LTL or pallet format depending on the cargo's parameters, the number of loading addresses and delivery conditions in Ukraine. If the shipment is collected from several Czech suppliers, this is factored into route planning and document coordination.

  4. 4. We coordinate the data and documents

    Before the route starts we check the data needed for transport and document support. If a customs stage is part of the agreed scenario, it is coordinated together with the logistics, not separately.

  5. 5. Transport and status updates

    During transport we report on cargo status and any changes that may affect the agreed plan. If agreement is needed to continue the route or delivery, we share the next steps.

  6. 6. We coordinate the customs stage and delivery in Ukraine

    After the cargo arrives in Ukraine, we coordinate the next steps within the agreed route and delivery to the unloading point.

Estimated transport time from Czechia

For typical road routes from Czechia to Ukraine, the estimated time depends on the loading city, cargo format, transport schedule, the border situation, security conditions and document support.

  • FTL — approximately 3–4 days;
  • LTL / consolidated cargo — approximately 5–6 days.

These timeframes are a planning guide. The actual timeframe is agreed for a specific route once cargo and transport conditions are confirmed.

What affects the transport cost

A fixed price doesn't reflect the real conditions of a specific delivery from Czechia to Ukraine. The cost depends on cargo parameters, loading addresses, the transport format, the need for special equipment and the route situation.

The quote is affected by:

  • fuel prices in the countries the route crosses and in Ukraine;
  • the security situation and possible restrictions linked to war-related risks in Ukraine;
  • transport format: FTL, LTL or pallet shipment;
  • distance between the loading city in Czechia and the delivery point in Ukraine;
  • loading at one or several suppliers;
  • weight, volume, dimensions, packaging and stacking possibility;
  • the need for oversized transport, ADR transport, a temperature regime or another special format;
  • transport availability on a specific route;
  • the scope of document coordination within the transport.

That's why we don't publish a universal “price from”. The quote is built on the specific delivery's parameters once the route, cargo and loading conditions are confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

How many pallets count as LTL, and when do I need a full truck?

The number of pallets matters, but it's not the only factor. We also look at their dimensions, total weight, volume, stacking possibility, cargo type, route addresses and delivery schedule. If the batch fills a significant part of the vehicle or needs a dedicated truck, FTL may fit better. For a smaller batch, LTL or a consolidated format is considered.

Can you arrange collection from several Czech suppliers?

Yes, this option can be considered for a specific delivery. For a preliminary estimate, send the loading cities or addresses, each batch's parameters, the readiness date and the delivery point in Ukraine. This data affects the route logic, transport format and quote.

What data is needed to calculate the cost?

We need the loading point in Czechia, the delivery address or city in Ukraine, product description, number of pallets or items, weight, volume or dimensions, packaging type, readiness date, and temperature, ADR or special transport requirements, if any.

How long does a quote take to prepare?

Preparing a quote depends on how complete the source data is, route complexity, cargo format and whether transport availability needs checking. To get an accurate quote without extra back-and-forth, provide precise batch and route parameters.

Can you transport non-standard, oversized or ADR cargo?

Such requests are assessed individually. We need precise dimensions, weight, loading method, photos or a technical specification, and for ADR cargo, the hazard class and related requirements. The possible transport format is determined based on this data.

Can you transport food, medical or energy-related goods?

Transport of food, medical or energy-related goods is assessed individually, taking into account the cargo's parameters, the required transport conditions and documents.
