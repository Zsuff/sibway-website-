Import Freight Transport from Germany to Ukraine for Business

Sibway Logistics arranges import freight transport from Germany to Ukraine for companies buying goods, components, equipment and production materials from European suppliers. We handle full truckloads (FTL), consolidated cargo (LTL) and pallet shipments.

We match the transport format to your batch's parameters, loading conditions and route to Ukraine. During transport we report on cargo status and any changes that may affect the agreed plan.

Who this service is for

Transport from Germany to Ukraine is designed for businesses buying goods, materials, components or technical solutions from German manufacturers, suppliers or distributors.

The service suits:

  • importers of goods and technical solutions from Germany;
  • manufacturing companies needing components, raw materials or equipment;
  • distributors restocking their warehouses;
  • companies buying from one or several suppliers;
  • procurement and logistics specialists arranging one-off or regular deliveries;
  • businesses that need FTL, LTL or pallet delivery.

What we transport

FTL — full truckload

FTL Germany–Ukraine is a format where a dedicated vehicle is arranged for a single delivery. It suits large batches, industrial supplies, regular runs, goods for distribution, equipment and cargo that needs its own route planning.

A full truckload can make sense for reasons beyond volume alone — a dedicated vehicle may be needed because of dimensions, packaging specifics, the number of loading points or the delivery schedule.

LTL — consolidated cargo

LTL, or consolidated cargo, is a format for batches that don't need a whole vehicle. The cargo travels together with compatible commercial shipments, and the route is planned around its parameters.

A consolidated format can work well for pallets, spare parts, components, warehouse goods, equipment batches and other cargo that doesn't fill a full vehicle.

Pallet shipments

If the cargo is prepared on pallets, the quote needs their number, dimensions, weight, height, packaging type and loading readiness. This data helps determine whether LTL, FTL or another transport format fits.

Equipment transport from Germany. Equipment, technical assemblies, components or non-standard cargo need a preliminary assessment of their parameters. Send us the available technical data — we'll assess which transport format may suit your delivery. If a technical specification is available, it also helps to note the weight, dimensions, loading method and packaging requirements.

How the transport works

  1. 1. You send a transport request

    Specify the city or loading address in Germany, the delivery location in Ukraine, the cargo description and the expected readiness date.

  2. 2. We clarify the cargo and route parameters

    To agree the transport format we clarify weight, volume, dimensions, number of pallets or items, packaging type, loading conditions, number of suppliers and the need for regular deliveries.

  3. 3. We agree the transport format

    For shipments from Germany we agree an FTL, LTL or pallet format based on the batch's parameters, loading addresses and the need for a dedicated or consolidated vehicle. If the cargo is collected from several suppliers, this is factored into route planning and document coordination.

  4. 4. We coordinate the data and documents

    Before the route starts we check whether the necessary data for transport and document support is in place. If a customs stage is part of the agreed scenario, it is coordinated together with the logistics, not separately.

  5. 5. Transport and status updates

    During transport we report on cargo status and any changes that may affect the agreed plan. If a decision is needed from the client, we agree the next steps within the route.

  6. 6. We coordinate the customs stage and delivery in Ukraine

    After the cargo arrives in Ukraine, we coordinate the next steps within the agreed route and delivery to the unloading point.

What affects the transport cost

A fixed rate doesn't reflect the real conditions of a specific import transport from Germany to Ukraine. The cost depends on cargo parameters, the loading point, the transport format and the route situation at the time of planning.

The quote is affected by:

  • fuel prices in the countries along the route, including Europe and Ukraine;
  • the security situation and war-related risks that may affect logistics in Ukraine;
  • loading format: dedicated FTL truck, consolidated LTL cargo or pallet shipment;
  • distance between addresses in Germany and Ukraine;
  • the number of loading points, if cargo is collected from several suppliers;
  • weight, volume, dimensions, packaging and product specifics;
  • the need for special transport or extra planning;
  • transport availability on a specific route;
  • the scope of document coordination within the transport.

That's why we don't state a conditional “price from” that may have nothing to do with your delivery. Instead, we quote the route based on your cargo's actual data and share the result once transport parameters are confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

How many pallets count as LTL, and when do I need a full truck?

The number of pallets matters, but it's not the only factor. We also look at their dimensions, height, total weight, volume, cargo type, route addresses and delivery schedule. If the batch fills a significant part of the vehicle or needs dedicated planning, FTL may fit better. For a smaller batch, LTL or consolidated transport is considered.

Can cargo be collected from several supplier addresses in Germany?

Yes, this is assessed for each request individually. Send the loading addresses or cities, cargo parameters, readiness of each batch and the delivery address in Ukraine. This lets us plan the route and calculate the transport correctly.

What data is needed to calculate the cost?

We need the loading point in Germany, the delivery address or city in Ukraine, product description, number of pallets or items, weight, volume or dimensions, packaging type, readiness date and any extra information on delivery regularity or special requirements.

How long does a quote take to prepare?

Preparing a quote depends on how complete the source data is, route complexity, cargo format and whether transport availability needs checking. To get an accurate quote without extra back-and-forth, provide precise batch and route parameters.

Can you transport non-standard or oversized cargo?

Such requests are assessed individually. We need precise dimensions, weight, loading method, photos or a technical specification. We'll assess which transport format may fit given the cargo parameters, loading conditions and route to Ukraine.

Can you transport equipment from Germany to Ukraine?

Yes, this depends on the equipment's characteristics, packaging, dimensions, weight and loading conditions. Send us the available technical data — we'll assess which transport format may suit your delivery.
