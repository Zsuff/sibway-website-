Logistics audit

We will conduct a full analysis of your logistics and identify ways to reduce costs. We optimise routes, delivery times and logistics processes for your business.

  • Ukraine — Kyiv, Lviv, Kharkiv, Odesa, Dnipro, Zaporizhzhia, Vinnytsia, Chernivtsi, Mykolaiv, Rivne and other cities
  • Poland — Warsaw, Kraków, Wrocław, Poznań, Gdańsk
  • Germany — Berlin, Hamburg, Munich, Frankfurt, Cologne
  • Czechia — Prague, Brno, Ostrava
  • Other EU countries — on an individual-request basis

We work with companies of every size, from small businesses to large enterprises.

Pricing factors

The cost of a logistics audit depends on:

  • Business size — number of logistics processes to be analysed
  • Geography — number of countries and routes to be analysed
  • Logistics complexity — number of suppliers, warehouses and routes
  • Depth of analysis — high-level review or detailed assessment
  • Additional services — implementation support and ongoing consulting

Required documents

Minimum package:

  • Contracts with logistics partners
  • Invoices and bills for logistics services
  • CMR consignment notes
  • Customs declarations, if applicable

Additional documents, if required

  • Warehouse reports
  • Delivery reports
  • Delivery-time data
  • Cargo and route information

Working example

Logistics audit for an electronics distributor

Client
A Kyiv-based distributor of electronics and household appliances
Challenge
High logistics costs, delivery delays and an unclear cost structure

Solution

  • Analysed six months of logistics data
  • Identified three key problems: inefficient routes, lack of consolidation and customs delays
  • Proposed route optimisation, cargo consolidation and changes to logistics partners

Result: 28% reduction in costs; delivery times reduced by 2 days; improved transparency of logistics processes.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a logistics audit take?

A standard audit takes 7–14 days, depending on the size of the business and logistics complexity.

What data is needed for an audit?

Contracts with logistics partners, invoices, CMR notes, as well as data on costs and delivery times.

Do you guarantee cost reduction?

We guarantee that we will identify ways to reduce costs. The specific percentage depends on the current situation.

Do you help implement recommendations?

Yes. We can support implementation and provide ongoing consultations.

How does payment work?

Payment is made by invoice to a bank account. Prepayment or payment after receiving the report may be available.

Do you work with private individuals?

Logistics audits are provided for businesses: legal entities and sole proprietors.
