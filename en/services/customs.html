Cargo customs clearance

We support customs clearance for import and export shipments between Ukraine and the EU. We help prepare documents, classify goods and complete customs procedures.

Get a quoteCall us

Clearance geography

Sibway Logistics supports customs clearance for:

  • Imports into Ukraine — from EU countries, China, Turkey and the USA
  • Exports from Ukraine — to EU countries, the United Kingdom and Turkey
  • Transit — through Ukraine to EU countries and in the opposite direction

We work with the main customs checkpoints in Ukraine, including Kyiv, Lviv, Yahodyn, Rava-Ruska, Krakovets, Uzhhorod, Chop, Odesa and Mykolaiv.

What we offer

Document preparation

Customs declarations

Customs consulting

Customs duty rates, exemptions and restrictions. Support in complex cases.

Liaison with customs authorities, resolution of disputed issues and faster procedures.

Frequently asked questions

Which documents are required for customs clearance?

An invoice, packing list, contract and, where necessary, certificate of origin. We provide the full list after reviewing your cargo.

How long does customs clearance take?

The standard timeframe is 1–3 days for imports and 1–2 days for exports. Expedited clearance takes one day.

Do you help with goods classification?

Yes. Our brokers determine the UKTZED code and verify it before submitting the declaration.

Which customs payments apply?

Customs duty, VAT and, where applicable, excise duty. Rates depend on the UKTZED code and country of origin.

Do you work with private individuals?

Yes. We support customs clearance for individuals importing goods.

How does payment for services work?

Payment is made by invoice to a bank account. Prepayment or post-payment may be available by agreement.

Need a customs clearance consultation?

Submit an enquiry or contact us by phone or messenger. We will help arrange customs clearance for your cargo.
