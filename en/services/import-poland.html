Import Freight Transport from Poland to Ukraine for Business

Sibway Logistics arranges freight transport from Poland to Ukraine for importers, distributors, manufacturers and procurement specialists. We match the transport format to your shipment: full truckload (FTL), consolidated cargo (LTL) or a pallet shipment.

The service is built for commercial cargo where a predictable process, a clear quote and status updates along the route matter.

Who this service is for

Transport from Poland to Ukraine suits businesses importing goods, materials or components that need organised road transport.

We work with:

  • importers and distributors;
  • manufacturing companies bringing in raw materials, components or equipment;
  • procurement specialists arranging deliveries from Polish suppliers;
  • companies with regular or one-off commercial shipments;
  • businesses that need a pallet, LTL or full truckload format.

What we transport

FTL — full truckload

FTL suits cargo that fills a significant part of the vehicle, or a shipment that needs a dedicated truck. This format works well for large batches, regular deliveries, equipment, goods for distribution and other cargo better kept separate from other clients' shipments.

For the quote we take into account weight, dimensions, number of pallets, loading type, route points and cargo readiness.

LTL — consolidated cargo

LTL, or consolidated cargo, suits shipments that don't need a full vehicle. It travels together with compatible commercial batches, and the route is planned around your cargo's parameters.

The cost of this format is set by your own batch's parameters: number of items or pallets, weight, volume, route and loading conditions. LTL is often used for pallets, product batches, components, packaging, equipment or regular warehouse restocking.

Pallet shipments

If the goods are prepared on pallets, choosing the right format needs their number, weight, dimensions, loading/unloading addresses and the readiness date.

Based on this data we agree whether FTL, LTL or another pallet-shipment scenario fits best.

How the transport works

  1. 1. You send a request

    Tell us where to collect the cargo in Poland, where to deliver it in Ukraine, what is being shipped and the batch's parameters.

  2. 2. We clarify the cargo parameters

    For the quote we need weight, volume, number of pallets, packaging type, cargo readiness, loading/unloading conditions and whether it's a one-off or regular shipment.

  3. 3. We agree the transport format

    Once cargo and route parameters are confirmed, we arrange transport in the format that fits your shipment: a dedicated truck, consolidated cargo or a pallet shipment.

  4. 4. We verify the data and documents for the route

    Before transport starts we confirm the cargo data and document support needed. If a customs stage is required, it is coordinated as part of the overall logistics process.

  5. 5. We arrange transport and report on status

    During transport we report on cargo movement and any changes that may affect the agreed plan. If a decision is needed from the client, we agree the next steps within the route.

  6. 6. We coordinate the customs stage and delivery in Ukraine

    After the cargo arrives in Ukraine, we coordinate the next steps within the agreed logistics scenario and delivery to the unloading point.

What affects the transport cost

The cost of import transport from Poland to Ukraine cannot be the same for every shipment. Even similar routes can have different prices due to batch parameters, loading points, route conditions and the availability of suitable transport.

The quote is affected by:

  • fuel prices in Ukraine and the European countries the route crosses;
  • the security situation and possible restrictions linked to war-related risks in Ukraine;
  • transport format: FTL, LTL or pallet shipment;
  • the actual distance between the loading point in Poland and the delivery point in Ukraine;
  • the number of loading or unloading addresses;
  • weight, volume, dimensions, packaging type and cargo specifics;
  • transport availability on a specific leg of the route at the time of planning;
  • the need for extra document coordination within the transport.

That's why we don't publish fixed rates that may not match a specific shipment's real conditions. Instead, we quote each request individually and share the result once the key route and cargo parameters are confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

How many pallets count as LTL, and when do I need a full truck?

There's no single number that fits every case. It depends on the number and size of the pallets, total weight, volume, cargo type, route addresses and delivery schedule. If the cargo fills a significant part of the vehicle or needs a dedicated truck, FTL may fit better. For a smaller batch, LTL or a consolidated format often works.

Can cargo be collected from several supplier addresses in Poland?

Yes, this scenario can be arranged. For a preliminary estimate, send all loading points, batch parameters, readiness at each address and the desired delivery address in Ukraine. This data affects the route and the quote.

What data is needed to calculate the cost?

For an initial quote we need the loading country and city, the delivery address or city in Ukraine, cargo description, number of pallets or items, weight, volume or dimensions, readiness date and information on delivery regularity, if any.

How long does a quote take to prepare?

Preparing a quote depends on how complete the data is, route complexity, cargo format and whether transport availability needs checking. Precise parameters from the start help avoid extra clarifications and speed up an accurate quote.

Can you transport non-standard or oversized cargo?

Each case is assessed individually. Send the cargo description, dimensions, weight, photos or technical specifications if available. We'll assess which transport format may fit given the cargo parameters, loading conditions and route to Ukraine.

Do you work with regular shipments from Poland?

Yes, Sibway Logistics can arrange regular import transport once shipment parameters, frequency and route are agreed in advance. For such requests it helps to share the expected schedule, volumes and cargo type upfront.
