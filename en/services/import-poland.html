Import freight transport from Poland to Ukraine for business

Sibway Logistics organises freight transport from Poland to Ukraine for importers, distributors, manufacturers and procurement teams. We match the transport format to your shipment: a full truckload (FTL), a groupage/LTL shipment, or a pallet consignment.

Who this service is for

This service is built for businesses that import goods from Poland to Ukraine and need organised road freight. We work with:

  • importers and distributors
  • manufacturers bringing in raw materials, components or equipment
  • procurement teams arranging deliveries from Polish suppliers
  • companies with regular or one-off commercial shipments
  • businesses shipping pallet, groupage (LTL) or full-truckload (FTL) cargo

Cargo types we carry

FTL — full truckload

Suits shipments that take up most of a truck, or when your consignment needs a dedicated vehicle. It's the right choice for large volumes, regular deliveries, equipment, distribution batches and other cargo that shouldn't be combined with other clients' shipments.

The quote accounts for more than weight — dimensions, pallet count, loading type, route points and cargo readiness all matter.

LTL — groupage cargo

Suits shipments that don't need a whole truck. Your cargo travels alongside other compatible commercial consignments, and the price is calculated around your shipment's own parameters.

Often chosen for pallets, product batches, components, packaging, equipment or regular stock replenishment, when a full truckload isn't practical.

Pallet shipments

If your goods are palletised, we help you choose the right delivery format based on pallet count, weight, dimensions, loading and unloading addresses, and delivery urgency.

To determine whether FTL, LTL or pallet shipping fits best, just share your cargo parameters — we'll propose a transport scenario for your specific request.

How the shipment works

  1. 1. You send a request

    Tell us where to collect the cargo in Poland, where to deliver it in Ukraine, what is being shipped and the shipment's parameters.

  2. 2. We confirm the cargo details

    For an accurate quote we confirm weight, volume, pallet count, packaging type, cargo readiness, loading/unloading conditions, and whether this is a one-off or a regular shipment.

  3. 3. We source transport through our partner network

    Sibway Logistics isn't tied to a single fleet. We bring in carrier partners for the specific route leg, cargo format and currently available transport.

  4. 4. We verify data and documents for the route

    Before the shipment starts, we align on the required cargo data and documentation. If a customs stage is needed as part of the shipment, it's coordinated as part of the overall logistics process.

  5. 5. We run the shipment and keep you informed

    Once the route starts, you receive updates on cargo movement and any important changes. If circumstances on the route could affect the plan, we stay in touch and agree next steps with you.

  6. 6. We coordinate customs and delivery in Ukraine

    Once the cargo arrives in Ukraine, we coordinate the remaining steps within the agreed logistics scenario and deliver to the unloading point.

What affects the cost of transport

The cost of import freight from Poland to Ukraine can't be the same for every shipment. Even two seemingly similar routes can differ in price because of shipment parameters, loading points, transport availability and route conditions. The quote depends on:

  • Fuel prices — in Ukraine and the European countries the route passes through
  • Security situation — possible restrictions linked to war-related risks in Ukraine
  • Transport format — full truckload (FTL), groupage (LTL) or a pallet shipment
  • Distance — the actual distance between the loading point in Poland and the delivery point in Ukraine
  • Number of addresses — for loading or unloading
  • Cargo parameters — weight, volume, dimensions, packaging type and other specifics
  • Transport availability — within our partner network on the specific route leg
  • Documentation coordination — any additional document coordination the shipment requires

That's why we don't publish fixed rates that might not match your specific shipment's conditions. Instead, we quote every request individually and share the result once we've confirmed the key route and cargo parameters.

Frequently asked questions

How many pallets count as LTL, and when do I need a full truck?

There's no single number that applies to every case. It depends not only on pallet count but also on their size, total weight, volume, cargo type, route addresses and the schedule you need. If the cargo takes up most of a truck or needs dedicated transport, FTL usually makes more sense. Smaller shipments often suit LTL or groupage.

Can you collect cargo from several supplier addresses in Poland?

Yes, that's possible to arrange. You'll need to specify all loading points, cargo readiness at each one, shipment parameters and the preferred delivery address in Ukraine. This affects the route and the quote, so it helps to share these details upfront.

What information do you need to calculate the price?

For an initial quote we need the country and city of loading, the delivery address or city in Ukraine, a cargo description, pallet or unit count, weight, volume or dimensions, the readiness date, and whether deliveries will be regular.

How long does it take to get a quote?

Quote turnaround depends on how complete the data is, route complexity, cargo format and whether transport availability needs confirming. Accurate details from the start help us avoid extra questions and prepare an accurate quote.

Can you transport non-standard or oversized cargo?

This is assessed case by case. Send us a cargo description, dimensions, weight, photos or a technical specification if available, and we'll check which transport format and route would fit.

Do you handle regular shipments from Poland?

Yes, Sibway Logistics can organise regular import shipments once volumes, frequency and route are agreed in advance. For these requests it helps to share an estimated schedule, volumes and cargo type upfront.
