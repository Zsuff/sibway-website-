Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

1. General provisions

Sibway Logistics (hereinafter referred to as the “Company”) respects your privacy and protects personal data in accordance with the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection” and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information.

2. Data we collect

We may collect the following personal data:

  • Contact information: first name, last name, email address and phone number
  • Company information: company name, EDRPOU code and address
  • Cargo information: cargo description, weight, volume and route
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and website visit data (cookies)

3. How we use your data

Your personal data is used to:

  • Process enquiries and provide services
  • Contact you regarding orders and services
  • Provide information about offers and news, subject to your consent
  • Improve the website and our services
  • Meet legal obligations

4. How we protect your data

We take all necessary measures to protect your personal data:

  • Use of secure communication channels (SSL/TLS)
  • Restricted access to personal data
  • Regular security-system updates
  • Employee training on data protection

5. Sharing data with third parties

We do not transfer your personal data to third parties, except to:

  • Logistics partners — to arrange transport
  • Customs authorities — for cargo customs clearance
  • Government authorities — where required by law
  • Service providers — hosting and email services, provided they maintain confidentiality

6. Cookies and analytics

Our website uses cookies to:

  • Improve website performance
  • Analyse traffic through Google Analytics
  • Personalise content

6.1 Managing cookies

You can configure your browser to disable cookies, but this may limit website functionality.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent to data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

8. Data retention

We retain your personal data for:

  • The duration of our cooperation — to perform contracts
  • 3 years — to comply with legal obligations
  • Until consent is withdrawn — for marketing communications

9. International data transfers

Your data may be transferred to EU countries to organise international transport. Such transfers are made in accordance with GDPR requirements.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The current version is always available on this page.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about personal data protection, please contact us using the phone number, email address or postal address published in the Contacts section of this website.
