About the company

An external logistics partner for B2B companies that need freight transport between Ukraine and Europe.

Who we are

Sibway Logistics is a Ukrainian freight-forwarding company. Our mission is to develop Ukraine’s logistics sector and support businesses on the international stage.

We don't just move cargo — we build logistics you can rely on. Our team takes on the organisation of transport, tariffs, documents and complex situations, so the client doesn't have to work through logistics chaos alone.

Where we work

Main directions:

  • Poland
  • Germany
  • Czechia
  • Romania
  • Turkey

Additional directions: Belgium, the Netherlands, the United Kingdom, France, Spain, Italy, Slovakia, Denmark, Norway, the Baltic states, Greece, Georgia, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Hungary and Bulgaria.

We work with all the main customs checkpoints in Ukraine, including Kyiv, Lviv, Yahodyn, Rava-Ruska, Krakovets, Uzhhorod, Chop, Odesa, Mykolaiv and others.

Cargo types

  • Standard cargo — common goods, equipment, materials
  • Hazardous cargo (ADR) — chemical substances, classes 2–9
  • Palletised cargo — euro-pallets, standardised cargo units
  • Bulk cargo — loose materials, grain, fertilisers
  • From 1 pallet — consolidated (LTL) shipments
  • From 50 kg — small business consignments
  • Oversized cargo — equipment, machinery, structures
  • Express delivery — urgent shipments, priority scheduling
